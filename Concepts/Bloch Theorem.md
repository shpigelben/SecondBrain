Two equations regarding Bloch electrons:
1. How do Bloch electrons move between collisions?
2. What exactly is the nature of collisions of Bloch electrons?

Drude assumed that electrons collide the lattice ions, but this assumption cannot be reconciled with the huge mean free paths of electrons in metals. It also failed to account for the dependence of the MFP on temperature. In fact the existence of lattice ions in itself does not contribute to resistance. If we adopt the wave nature of electrons, it can be shown that waves in a periodic media can interfere constructively as to not be perturbed by the lattice. That way, in a perfectly ordered lattice, with no impurities and no thermal vibrations, conductivity would be infinite. There are rather the scattering of Bloch electrons off impurities and phonons that are the main causes of resistance.
