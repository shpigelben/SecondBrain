1. Prepare cure with a certain volumetric ratio r
2. Volume and mass are given and therefore the effective density rho_eff.
3. Measure T = t_pressure - t_current for every height H.
4. After each measurement peal a 2 mm layer and repeat step 3.
5. a graph of H vs T should be linear, and the estimated slope would be the speed of sound c_eff.
6. The effective impedance is Z_eff = rho_eff*c_eff.
7. At the end of this process we have 5 layers of the composite material.
   Measuring the impedance of each can give an estimate of the level of 
   homogeneity of the overall layer (|Z_eff^measured - Z_eff^average| and Z_eff^std).
8. The entire process should be done for volumetric ratios (r) from 5%-60% in steps of 5%
   The result should be plotted Z_eff vs r and the deviation and std as functions of r.
