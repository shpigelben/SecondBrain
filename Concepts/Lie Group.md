A finite - continuous [[Groups|group]] $\big(\{ \tau_{i} \},*\big)$ exists in a continuous manifold. If we demand that the manifold is also _differentiable_ at any point, we get a __Lie Group__. A smooth manifold locally resembles [[euclidean space]]. Lie groups are useful in that regard, since the global group can be "replaced" by the local linearized version - the tangent space that is it's associated [[Lie Algebra]].
