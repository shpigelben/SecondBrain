The microcanonical ensemble represents an __isolated__ system with constant energy, constant particles and constant volume. And so a microcanonical ensemble contains many virtual copies of the system in all microstates corresponding to a macrostate $(U,X,N)$.

A system in [[Thermodynamic Equilibrium]] satisfies the __Ergodic Principle__ which states that all microstates of the system are equally distributed. Thus, a system in a macrostate $(U,X,N)$ that emerged from a __multiplicity__, $\Gamma(U,X,N)$, of microstates has probability $\displaystyle \frac{1}{\Gamma}$ of being in any microstate.
