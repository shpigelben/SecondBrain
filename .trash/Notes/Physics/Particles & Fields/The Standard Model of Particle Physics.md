## Units in the Standard Model
natural units $\hbar = c = 1$
$$[c]= \frac{[L]}{[T]}\equiv1 \Longrightarrow [L]=[T]$$
$$ [E]=\hbar\omega = \frac{1}{[T]}=\frac{1}{[L]} $$
from relativistic mass-energy relation
$$ E=\sqrt{m^{2}c^{4}+p^{2}c^{2}}\leadsto\sqrt{m^{2}+p^{2}} $$
we get that $[E]=[M]=[P]$

Therefore everything can be measured in terms of energy, specifically electron-volts, eV.
$$\boxed{\begin{align*}
1\text{eV}&=1.6\times10^{-19}\text{J} \\
e &= 1.6\times10^{-19}\text{C}
\end{align*}}$$
