#computation #information

The bit (contraction of *B*inary Dig*it* ) is the most basic unit of information in the digital world. The bit represents a [[Logical State]] with one of two possible values "$0$" or "$1$, in contrast with the [[Qubit]] that can be in a superposition of the two values.

- A group of binary digits is called a "bit string" or "bit array".
- A byte is 8 binary digits. (01110101)

A computer with $n$ bits exists in one of $2^n$ possible states
