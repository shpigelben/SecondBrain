Mr Norris, was sitting on the couch guessing his way through a crossword puzzle. It was a rainy day, although not quite as chilly as one might have expected from the picture of dancing trees beneath the sooty skies, which were screened through the living room's window.

He gave a long sigh, setting aside his unfinished puzzle after realising he made a mistake. Taking a sip from his lukewarm tea, he glanced at Mrs Norris, who was slouching on the sofa beside him, flipping through her phone as she would usually do throughout most of the day. He loved Mrs Norris, or at least he thought he was - after all, they have been married for 7 years now - but he was looking at her with a look of deep dissatisfaction.

To be fair, as far as he recalls, he has been unssatisfied for most of his life. At his job as a high school teacher he was average at most.

  

Mr Norris felt old, acted old, and was reminded - with good natured remarks -  of this inclination of his by his associates.
