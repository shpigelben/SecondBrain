# Speed of Sound

$$
c_{\small s} = \sqrt{\left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial \rho}\right)_{s}}
$$

If the sound wave propagates [[adiabatic process|adiabatically]] we can make use of the fact that $P\propto V^{-\Gamma}\propto \rho^{\Gamma}$ to arrive at the general expression for such a [[Acoustic Waves|sound wave]]

$$
c_{s}= \sqrt{\frac{\Gamma P}{\rho}}
$$

If the medium in which the wave propagates is consisted of [[Classical Ideal Gas|ideal gas]] then the speed of sound can be more specifically be written as

$$
c_{s} = \sqrt{\frac{\Gamma k_{\small B} T}{\braket{m}}}
$$

In which case, one has to know the mass of the gas constituents, it's adiabatic index and its general temperature in order to calculate the speed of sound in it.
