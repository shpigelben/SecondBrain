- For a planar coil with a diameter of $D = 30 \ \text{[mm]}$  and a wire thickness of $d=0.5 \ \text{[mm]}$ (including insulation), the number of wires is given by $$ N = \eta\left(\frac{D}{d}\right)^2 \approx 3240 $$with $\eta\approx 0.9$ for tightly packed wires in an approximate hexagonal lattice. When wrapped around a wheel of $R=100 \ \text{[mm]}$ the length of each loop is $\pi R$ and the total length is $L = N\pi R \approx 1020 \ [m]$.
- ==Even though the wires are insulated from one another, each still experiences its own change in magnetic flux and thus generated an azimuthal current (around the axis of symmetry of each wire) which in turn generates heat==
