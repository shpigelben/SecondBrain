Books, why do I read them ? Mainly, escapism. Despite being more demanding than watching a movie or a TV series, getting immersed in a book is an experience quite unlike any other. 

With a movie, every viewer sees exactly the same scenery, character hears the same sounds and voices and is given the portrayal of the atmosphere as intended by the director of the movie.

When reading a book two people can imagine all those things in very different ways.

  

Some books I know I’ll reread in the future, and so I keep them in my library, and some I finish and give away sell or donate. Some I get rid off and repurchase them when I realize that I want to read them again. 

  

My criteria for keeping a book are very strict since I like physical books and room is scarce.

  

From an early age I enjoyed reading books, but it was only recently that I began reading in bulks never missing a day of reading. Why is that ? Not because I want to get super smart or anything. Most books give you either superficial knowledge or none at all, only few really take you on a journey of learning and understanding.

The reason for reading everyday is simply because I love books and there are simply too many.

  

Sure there are the added benefits of increased focus, faster reading speed and the chance to brag about reading such and such, but these are by products of reading and not the reason for doing it.
