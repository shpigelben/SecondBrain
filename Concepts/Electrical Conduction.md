**Electrical Conductivity** ($\sigma$) is a macroscopic quality of materials. It describes the extent to which substances allow the passage of charged particles (usually [[electrons]]) through them.

On the other hand there is the **Electrical Resistivity** ($\rho$) which depicts how strongly a material resists [[electric current]]. Resistivity is the reciprocal of conductivity

- conductivity -  is the inherent intensive property of a material
- conductance -  is the extensive property that depends on the dimensions of the material.
___
# Materials
substances with good electric conductivity are usually called [[metals]], whereas those with poor electric conductivity are called [[insulators]] or [[dielectrics]].
