A Markov-chain (or a Markov process) is a [[Stochastic Processes|stochastic model]] that describes a sequence of possible events $(X_{1},X_{2}\dots)$ in which the probability for the occurrence of a certain event depends only on the previous event ([[Markov Property]])

Markov chains can describe the sequence of either discrete or continuous random variable (state space) and can describe a sequence in which transitions between states occur in discrete jumps or in continuous intervals. All four of these combinations have appropriate phenomena they portray
