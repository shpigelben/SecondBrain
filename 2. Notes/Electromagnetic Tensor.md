The electromagnetic tensor is an __anti-symmetric__ tensor that holds the entire information about certain configurations of field. It is given by
$$F^{\mu\nu}= \partial^{\mu}A^{\nu}-\partial^{\nu}A^{\mu}=\begin{pmatrix}0 & -E_{x} & -E_{y} & -E_{z} \\ E_{x} & 0 & -B_{z} & B_{y} \\ E_{y} & B_{z} & 0 & -B_{x} \\ E_{z} & -B_{y} & B_{x} & 0\end{pmatrix}$$
Where $A^{\mu}$ is the [[Four Vector|four-potential]] and $\partial^{\mu}$ is the four-gradient. Through the EM tensor, all of four of [[Maxwell Equations]] can be written compactly using the four-current
$$\large\partial_{\mu}F^{\mu \nu}=j^{\nu}$$
