# Miserable Interview at Applied
**job description** - part of a research group devoted to creating better inspection and metrology tools for semi-conductor production.

**what went right** - Tal said that he was impressed with how well I remembered the details of the BCS hamiltonian, but I think that he was being generous since he felt bad for me.

**what went wrong (chronologically)** - 
- Lack of familiarity with the company and of general related knowledge
	- What the company does broadly
	- General knowledge regarding wafers, semi-conductors and so on.
	- **Specific question:** What is the OOM in which impurities and defects begin to effect the quality of a semi conductor, or a junction?
	- Question about the inspection rate of 300mm wafer with 20nm resolution.
- Bad Presentation of Project
	- Chose to talk about the BCS project. Maybe should have gone with the Digital Holography lab.
	- Difficulty establishing predominant causes for resistance in conductors.
		- Is it collisions with atoms? with other electrons? if so by which mechanism? ==In general, a perfect lattice is a perfect conductor. Any deviation from that is a contributor to resistance. In room temperature, phonons are thermal distortions  of the lattice that constitute the main contribution for resistance. Other processes like scattering from defects or impurities also play a role in the resistance of conductors==
		- Couldn't answer about how one measures mean free path of an electron experimentally?
- Inability to answer simple questions about lenses
	- How far from the lens is the formations of an image.
	- Assuming the lens is cut in half, how does it change the focal point of lens? Is there still imaging?
		- finding the phase $\Delta \phi(y)$ of a lens cut in half

**General things to improve & takeaways** 
- Having a better narrative when presenting myself.
- Being more familiar with the company and general relevant knowledge.
- Being able to talk fluently and deeply about every project presented in the CV or any project I choose to talk about.
