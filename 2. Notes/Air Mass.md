Air mass is absorption-weighted path a ray of light takes in a medium.
The extinction of light as it travels through the atmosphere is dependent on it's density.
A denser atmosphere contains more particles per unit volume and consequently absorbs and scatters more of the light passing through it. The density of the atmosphere is position dependent (more specifically, it is dependent on the distance from the center of the Earth)
