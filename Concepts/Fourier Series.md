**The complex Fourier series** of a periodic function $f(t)$ is given by
$$\hat{f}(\omega) = \frac{1}{T} \sum\limits_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} f_{n} {\large e}^{-i \omega nt}$$
where $\omega=\frac{2\pi}{T}$ and $T$ is the period of the function.
