What is computer graphics?
> The use of computer to synthesize sensory information

Oftentimes graphics is associated only with the visual aspect. Which kind of makes sense since graphics' etymology is 99% visual related.

Computer vision - the inverse? Takes sensory input from the world and converts it to information.
