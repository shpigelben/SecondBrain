A system in _Thermal Equilibrium_  - Is a system, all subsystems of which are of equal temperature

A system in _Mechanical Equilibrium_  - Is a system, all subsystems of which exert equal forces on each other.

A system in _Thermodynamic Equilibrium_ - is a system that is both in thermal and mechanical equilibrium.
