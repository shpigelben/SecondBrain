Eddington Finkelstein are another set of coordinates that demonstrate how $r=0$ is a true singularity, whereas $r=2M$ is a coordinate singularity that appears in spherical coordinates
$$(t,r,\theta, \varphi) \longmapsto (v,r,\theta, \varphi)$$$$ t = v - \left(r+2M\log{\left|\frac{r}{2M}-1\right|}\right) \tag{1}$$
[[Schwarzschild Geometry|Schwarzschild Metric]] in the new coordinates is
$$ dS^{2}=-\left(1-\frac{2M}{r}\right)dv^{2} + 2dvdr + r^{2}(d\theta^{2}+sin^{2}\theta d\varphi^{2}) $$
___
#### Light Cones 
Null trajectories in the radial direction require that - $d\theta = d\varphi = 0$ and that $dS^{2}=0$ which leaves us with the following
$$-{\small\left(1-\frac{2M}{r}\right)}dv^{2} + 2dvdr = 0$$
There are two possible solutions for a general radius to this differential equation. one is $dv=0$ which means $v=\text{const}$, and so by the definition of $v$ in $(1)$, to remain constant, as time increases the radius must get smaller.

Another possible solution is 
$$ \frac{dv}{dr}=\frac{2}{1- \frac{2M}{r}} $$
