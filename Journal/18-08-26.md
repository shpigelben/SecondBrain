## Strength

- decided to try and change the circuit to RIR (reps in reserve) meaning that each set is done until only 2-3 reps are left in reserve. Should be better both for BDNF production and strength. Meaning reps will decrease as sets progress, but the benefit should be greater.

| pullups                                 | 14    | 10    | 8     | 32    |

- started really shaking in round two after the elevated pushups.
- At the the third round felt like I couldn't do the deadlifts and the lateral raises, not because those muscles specifically felt sore, but because I was already done for. Wanted only to finish with lateral raises.

- Lastly, if I am to incorporate this RIR, it should be only on weekend days where I only workout and don't skip later in the day like today
