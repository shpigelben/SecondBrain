The Einstein tensor is a combination of the Ricci tensor and the Ricci scalar
$$ \large G_{\mu \nu} \equiv R_{\mu \nu} - \frac{1}{2}R \ g_{\mu \nu} $$
The Einstein tensor can be derived from the __Bianchi Identity__

$$ \begin{align*}
&\nabla_{\alpha}R_{\beta \gamma \mu \nu} + \nabla_{\beta}R_{\gamma \alpha \mu \nu} + \nabla_{\gamma}R_{\alpha \beta \mu \nu} =0\\\\
& \Downarrow \ g^{\beta \mu}\\\\
& \nabla_{\alpha} R_{\gamma \nu} + \nabla^{\mu}R_{\gamma \alpha \mu \nu} -\nabla_\gamma R_{\alpha \nu}=0\\\\
& \Downarrow \ g^{\gamma \nu} \\\\
& \nabla_{\alpha}R - \nabla^{\mu}R_{\alpha \mu} - \nabla^{\nu}R_{\alpha \nu} = 0\\
& \\
&\nabla^{\mu}g_{\mu \alpha}R - 2\nabla^{\mu}R_{\mu \alpha}=0 \\
& \\
&\nabla^{\mu} \left(R_{\mu \nu} - \frac{1}{2}Rg_{\mu \nu} \right)=0
\end{align*}$$
