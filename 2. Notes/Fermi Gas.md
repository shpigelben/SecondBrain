$N$ non-interacting fermions are in an [[particle in a box|3D infinite square well]]
$$V(x,y,z)=\begin{cases}
0, &x,y,z \in \left( -\frac{L}{2}, \frac{L}{2} \right) \\
\infty,  & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}$$
Inside the box, the fermions behave as free particles and their energy is given by
$$E = \frac{\hbar^{2} k^{2}}{2m} = \frac{\hbar^{2}\pi^{2}}{2mL^{2}}\Big(n_{x}^{2}+n_{y}^{2}+n_{z}^{2}\Big)$$

$$k=\sqrt{ k_{x}^{2} + k_{y}^{2} +k_{z}^{2}} \quad \quad k_{i}=\frac{\pi}{L}n_{i}$$
