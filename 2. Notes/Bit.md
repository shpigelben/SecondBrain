#information 

The bit (contraction of **Bi**nary Dig**it** ) is the most basic unit of information in the digital world. The bit represents a Logical State which can be found in either of two possible values: "$0$" or "$1$ (a [[Qubit]], on the other hand, can be in a superposition of the two values).

- A group of binary digits is called a "bit string" or "bit array".
- A byte is 8 binary digits. (01110101)

A computer with $n$ bits exists in one of $2^n$ possible states
