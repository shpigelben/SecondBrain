Say we have a $2\times 2$ and $3\times 3$ matrix representations for a certain group.
We can create a $\dim 2\oplus 3$ reducible representation with a [[Block Diagonal Matrices| block diagonal matrix]]. 

Since multiplication of two block diagonal matrices corresponds to multiplication of the blocks, and assuming we have a multiplication table for the blocks we get a new representation

$$\large\left(\begin{array}{c|c}
2\times 2 & ~ \\
\hline
~ & 3\times 3
\end{array}\right) \cdot
\left(\begin{array}{c|c}
2'\times 2' & ~ \\
\hline
~ & 3'\times 3'
\end{array}\right) = 
\left(\begin{array}{c|c}
\tilde2\times \tilde2 & ~ \\
\hline
~ & \tilde3\times \tilde3
\end{array}\right)
$$
