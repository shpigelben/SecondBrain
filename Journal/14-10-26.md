Had a dream about Einav. It was a group meeting of some sorts. I was reading or otherwise occupied (as is the case with me my dreams tend to not be very detailed). I knew she also attended the meeting and part of me was mildly excited for that. At a certain point, while I was still occupied doing something I cannot recall she appeared next to me. I remember thinking fondly of her looks and manner. Can’t recall the exchange that we made but I do recall it being positive and that it concluded with a warm if not a sensual hug. A very short dream during which I woke up.

I’ve been dreaming about her every couple of months or weeks for as long as I can remember myself. Usually it is in a sensual or romantic context. As always, never too explicit or detailed, but the general gist is implied in the dream. On the one hand it is weird that she appears in my dream this regularly as I can’t remember that last time I’ve seen or interacted with her (it’s been years). I also dream about Adi and much more often in those contexts which does make sense as we live and share a life together.

For context Einav was my first relationship. Too big a word for romantic interaction that lasted around several months when I was 14 and that climaxed at my (and I think hers) first kiss. This alone makes her take a free space in my mind, but more than that I think its the combination of the lack of relationships thereafter for the longest of times (a true period of loneliness of the romantic kind) and the fact that I maintained long lasting feelings for her even after we broke up. To be clear this is definitely no longer the case, but what I think she is, is the representation or the amalgamation of my romantic muse. Even though I don’t think about her on a daily basis or am attracted to her, I think that in some way she represents some sort of ideal realization of romanticism for me. Not that being with her would accomplish that, but that is probably how it was for my 14 year old self and for good or bad I’ve been carrying that notion with me and probably will keep on having it around.
