## Learning Is Misunderstood

A lot of educators praise the power of repetition as a golden ticket to learning and forming knowledge and memories. But repetition in and of itself is not nearly enough. __The penny test__ in which people are asked to identify the correct image of a penny out of dozen incorrect images reveals that even the repetition in using common items on a daily basis for years does not result in a meaningful memory as most people usually fail these kinds of tests

## Early Evidence

In a study, two groups of people were asked to look on a list of pairs of nouns and numbers (moon-11, television-2, and so on) for a set period of time. They were not asked to memorize anything.

In the second step of the study the two groups were now given another list of such pairs. One group received the exact same list from the first phase, while another received a new list with different nouns.

The remarkable result of this study was that the learning curve for the two groups was statistically identical. This implies that prior exposure does not play a major role in learning, and that __intentionality is key__. 

Rereading is a time-consuming strategy that yields negligible results when done with little time lapse in between.

## Illusion of Knowing

A rising familiarity with a text and fluency in reading it gives a false sense of mastery, but the repeating of phrases from an essay is not an indicative of true understanding of the gravity and implication of those phrases.

The *illusion of mastery* is an example of poor [[metacognition]] (what we know about what we know).

1. known-knowns (good)
2. known-unknowns (better)
3. unknown-unknowns (good to be aware of)
4. unknown-knowns (get yourself checked. JK)

# The Testing Effect

Learning can be thought of the act of interrupting [[Forgetting Curve|forgetting]]. The power of retrieval is known among psychologists as _the testing effect_. To be most effective, retrieval has to be repeated in a spaced manner so that the act of remembering does not become automatic but requires some mental effort.
