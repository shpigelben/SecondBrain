Developing the electromagnetic interaction amplitudes by [[Virtual Particles & The Propagator|summing over all time orderings]] and later by [[The Electromagnetic Interaction|summing over all possible polarization states]] of the virtual photon of the electromagnetic interaction, for each process is time consuming and unproductive. Luckily Feynman rules for QED which are derived from QFT allow us to easily derive the matrix elements for any [[Feynman diagram|Feynman Diagrams]]

There are three main component for the Feynman diagrams - 
1. the initial and final fermion states
2. the propagator - the mediating virtual particle
3. a vertex factor at each interaction vertex

Each of these elements correspond to a _Feynman rule_
