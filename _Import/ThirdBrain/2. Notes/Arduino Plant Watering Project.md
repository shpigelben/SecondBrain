# Where does the automation comes in?
The arduino solves two problems at once:
1. it allows us to relatively accurately know the moisture level in the soil, using specialized sensors, rather than sticking out fingers in the soil and estimating
2. when required, the prototype automatically waters the thirsty plant. (We don't need to keep track of soil moisture and remember to water the plants)
