[[Huygens]] deflection formula
## Huygens Interpretation

$$
\Delta \phi_d = \Delta \phi_o + kd\cdot\theta 
$$
Where $\Delta \phi_o$ is the initial phase difference with which both rays arrive at the two slits, and $\Delta \phi_d$ is the phase shift at distance $d$ from the slit
$$\Delta \phi_d = 2\pi n \longrightarrow \theta =
\frac{2\pi n - \Delta \phi_0}{kd}$$
the above corresponds to constructive interference, where the peaks of the wave-functions produce a visible pattern  whose angular distribution is given by $\theta$. And
$$\Delta \phi_d = \pi +2\pi n \longrightarrow \theta =
\frac{\pi + 2\pi n - \Delta \phi_0}{kd}$$
which corresponds to destructive interference that produce troughs of the pattern at $d$

## Phase Accumulation Interpretation
It is a phenomenological fact that in the presence of electric and magnetic fields, the ray of lights are deflected from their course. This phenomenon can be explained with [[Phase Accumulation]]
$$
\phi_2 - \phi_1 = \Delta \phi = \Delta\Phi_E + \Delta\Phi_B
$$
Where 
$$ \Delta\Phi_E = (V_2 - V_1)\Delta t $$
and
$$\Phi_B = \int_{C_2} A(r)dr - \int_{C_1} A(r)dr = \oint A(r)dr$$
Such that
$$ \Delta \phi = (V_2 - V_1)\Delta t + \oint A(r)dr $$
