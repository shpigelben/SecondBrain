# Invariance of the Hamiltonian

# Under Translations
Translational Symmetry $\Leftrightarrow$ $[ \mathcal{H}, p ]=0$ $\Leftrightarrow$ $\mathcal{H}$ and $p$ are can be diagonalized in the same basis
$$
\braket{\tilde{x}|\hat{\mathcal{H}}|\tilde{x}} = \braket{x|\hat{D}^{\dagger}\hat{\mathcal{H}}\hat{D}|x'} = \braket{x|\tilde{\hat{\mathcal{H}}}|x'}
$$
inside $\mathcal{H}$ the potentials transform according to the algebraic description of translations
$$\tilde{V}(\hat{x}) \mapsto V(\hat{x}+a)$$
$$\tilde{A}(\hat{x}) \mapsto A(\hat{x}+a)$$
# Under Boosts
###  Boost
$\hat{p}$ generates change in $\ket{x}$
$$ e^{-ix_0\hat{p}}\ket{x} = \ket{x+x_0} $$
We call that __translations__ since it involves a spatial translation
$-\hat{x}$ generates change in $\ket{p}$
$$ e^{ip_0\hat{x}}\ket{p} = \ket{p+p_{0}} $$
We call that __boost__ since it involves boosting to a state with higher momentum
# Under a General Galilei Transformation
Translation + Rotation + Boost
The following $\hat{T}$ performs the translation and rotation of coordinates. The addition of $-v_0\hat{p}$ is the boost, the transformation to a frame with velocity $v_0$. The addition of $- \Omega_0\hat{L}_n$ is the transformation to a rotating frame with angular velocity $\Omega_0$
$$
\tilde{\mathcal{H}} = T^{\dagger}\mathcal{H}T -v_0\hat{p{}} - \Omega_0\hat{L}_n
$$
