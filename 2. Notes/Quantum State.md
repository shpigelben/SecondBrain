# Expectation Value

$$\begin{align*}
\braket{A} = \braket{\psi|A|\psi} &= \sum\limits_{n,m} c^{*}_{n}c_{m} \braket{n|A|m}\\
&\equiv \sum\limits_{n,m} \rho_{nm} A_{nm}\\
& = \text{Tr}(\rho A)
\end{align*} $$
$$\large\boxed{\begin{align*} \\ \quad
\braket{\hat{A}} = \text{Tr}(\rho\hat{A}) \quad \\\
\end{align*}}$$
