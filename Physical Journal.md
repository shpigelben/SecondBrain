This is the first time in years, perhaps ever, that I've taken means to work on my fitness in earnest and in an organized fashion. While I entertained the premise of human existence being divided into four separate pillars, namely the physical, the mental, the emotional and the spiritual (curtesy of the 'Chasing Mavericks' movie) I come to realize that, unsurprisingly, things are generally more involved. And after having read about 20% of Yukio Mishima's 'Sun & Steel' an idea captured my thoughts about how the mind, if really believed to be rooted in the physical (along with the emotional and spiritual aspects that stem from it) is some sort of derivative. This kind of understanding generate a new perspective. One that accepts one's physical nature as a basis to the mental, which itself is can be thought of a basis to the emotional and the spiritual (it would be interesting to check that against current scientific perspective). This puts a very large weight on the physical, and from that outset means one should first and foremost make sure that their physical self is tended for and the rest - which should be tended for nonetheless - are downstream from that and consequently benefit automatically from it. This is why I choose to begin this journey of personal development (although the popular term might make one dry heave) with the careful tending to the physical, in a slow, responsible and aware manner. I am looking for what is equivalent to Lev Landau's 'theoretical minimum'; a 'physical minimum' if you will. A set of things I find essential and that should be met on a daily basis to maintain the desired physical state that I seek and that support the desired mental, emotional and spiritual states that follow. This journal is my attempt at not only building a solid foundation that covers most of the **physical aspects**, but mainly at understanding those physical requisites that should be engaged with on a daily and weekly basis. One might try to define, using words, the main aspects of the physical

- strength
- endurance
- agility
- coordination
- mobility
- explosiveness

However, if one believes the physical to be the foundation at the bottom of the existential pyramid, one should concede that words and definitions might not be sufficient to capture all aspects of the physical and I am willing to accept that my understanding of it all might dramatically change. But for keeping things simple, I believe that the above list should serve as a lighthouse for the first part of the journey of physical self discovery as we venture to tend to each and every aspect with care.

> [!NOTE] Main (functional) goal
>  Cultivating the best possible, holistic fitness:
>   
>   I want to be **strong**, be able to move myself and things efficiently and gracefully. I want to have great **endurance**, be able to run and swim and rack for good distances with proper form. I want to develop a body that is **agile** and **explosive**, jump high and be able to fall properly. I want a good **coordination**, be able to move with rhythm and perform complex movements. I also want have great **mobility**, being flexible and strong in good ranges of motions rather than stiff. Lastly, I want to have a good **balance** and a good mind body connection. 



> [!NOTE] Secondary (aesthetic goal)
> Cultivating a defined (not too bulky) physique; current emphasis on:
> - wide & defined shoulders
> - defined obliques
> - defined quads & calves
> - good posture
