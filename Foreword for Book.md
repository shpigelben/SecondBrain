While this is a book about the physical nature of the human body, and how physical models capture neatly some of its working principles, needless to mention how complex everything is and how utterly impossible it would be to properly capture every aspect of this magnificent machinery, let alone the brain which is (perhaps) the seat of our conscious experience. The questions of how our consciousness emerges is perhaps the final frontier we will ever face as a civilization, perhaps we'll never know. Maybe there is not physical explanation for such phenomena that have to do with our souls. I'm not here to discuss the supernatural or the metaphysical, and I especially don't have the credentials for such explorations, nor is it the aim of this book whose sole purpose is to discuss the wonder of human body. How intricate and ingenious it is, even when considered through the lens of the feeble models we do possess that are able to describe some of its working principles.
