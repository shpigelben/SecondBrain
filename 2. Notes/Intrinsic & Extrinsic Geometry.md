Intrinsically, a trajectory will look the same on every object (since locally it is flat), but extrinsically trajectories will deviate slightly. One cannot distinguish such a thing intrinsically. It is only through the metric that we are able to deduct intrinsically trajectories, since the metric encapsulates the extrinsic geometry.
