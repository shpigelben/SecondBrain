__Cohesive energy of a crystal__ is the energy required to separate its components into natural free atoms at rest, at infinite separation with the same electronic configuration.

__Lattice energy__ is the energy required to its components ions into  free ions at rest at infinite separation.

* __cohesive__ relates to identical components that tend to hold together whereas __adhesive__ relates to different components that tend to hold together

$$
\begin{align*}
E_{\text{ionization}} &= E_{\small  Na^{+} + e^{-}} - E_{\small Na}\\
E_{\text{e-affinity}} &= E_{\small Cl +e^{-}} - E_{\small Cl^{-}}\\
E_{\text{cohesive}} &= E_{\small Na^{+} + Cl^{-} } - E_{\small NaCl}
\end{align*}
$$

$$\Delta E = E_{ion}-E_{aff}-E_{coh}$$
if $\Delta E<0$ then the reaction occurs

$$Na^{+} + Cl^{-} \to NaCl$$

# Crystals of Inert Gasses
Inert gases form the [[Bravais Lattice| simplest crystals]].
- electron distribution close to that of free atoms
- transparent insulators
- weakly bound
- low melting temperature
- very high ionization energy
- outermost energy shells of atoms are completely filled
	- distribution of electron charge in the free atom is spherically symmetric
