#note 

- Create notes with active recall in mind. Avoid mindlessly pouring content from one place to the other - even if there's benefit in the act in itself. When writing, plan for active engagement be it questions at the top of the article or what not.

Having the knowledge, neatly packed in ones own words is good, but not enough. Whenever available, upon revisiting or referencing material, it is best to first test oneself on the knowledge before revealing it.

> [!NOTE] 
> Obsidian is a good place to store forgotten cognitive and mental processes the one has gone through and got eroded with time and disuse.

Many times in our intellectual journey we go through mental processes that might later remain untouched due to irrelevance or what not. Our brain keeps and maintains whatever is useful to it. Still, the feeling that a good cognitive process might go to waste is saddening, and a lot if times we might discover that we need to recover something we once knew well but that has been eroded. Going through the same mental process for recovery of that idea might be a fruitful endeavor as always, but if we had a window into that study session in which that idea was encoded we might gain most of the benefit and save a lot of time.

___
- Note taking, for me, is more about the process and less about the knowledge base
- Knowledge base is important because sometimes you want to **do** instead of just **know**, and it is then that atomic and accessible knowledge at your fingertip is valuable.
- Still it is ok to want to **know** and even better **understand** (whatever that means) more and in such cases the **process** of constructing, summarizing and displaying ideas is more important.
- Taking that point of view, one must realize that the task of note taking or a **knowledge graving** (which I like better) is a never ending task. It is a never ending task that requires one to constantly interact, revise, rewrite and relink old ideas.

# Workflow
write a **temporary knowledge-dump** note that includes a lot of hierarchical sections. These sections will be later boiled down into cards on an obsidian canvas, linked and expanded upon. It is a good place to get a bird's eye view on the subject, reason and create links. After the relevant cards are combined they can be transformed into atomic conceptual notes that can be referenced from a relevant **MOC**

- It is useful to have information that is written and is distilled in a way that is familiar and reflects your own way of thinking. This saves time when trying to retrieve previously learned knowledge.
