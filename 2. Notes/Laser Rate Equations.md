$$\tau_{21}\gg \tau_{32} \Rightarrow N_{3}\ll N_{1},N_{2} \Rightarrow N_{1}+N_{2}\approx N_{tot}$$
$$\frac{ \partial N }{ \partial t } = -\underbrace{ (\gamma\sigma c)\phi N  }_{ stimulated }- \underbrace{ \frac{N+N_{{tot}}(\gamma-1)}{\tau _{f}} }_{ spontaneous }+\underbrace{ W_{p}(N_{tot}-N) }_{ pump }$$
$$\frac{ \partial \phi }{ \partial t } =c \sigma \phi N - \frac{\phi}{\tau_{c}}+S$$
$\tau_{c}$ is the decay time inside an optical resonator. At **lasing threshold** the photon density $\phi$ remains constant. And since $S\to 0$ we get that
$$N_{th}=\frac{1}{c \sigma \tau_{c}}$$
