A 10 minute routine done at work, usually at around 10:45  

- dynamic warmup for 2 minutes
- 30 pushups
- 8x2 side plank elevations
- 10 crab pushups
- 10 bicep curls with a chair
- 15x2 Cossack squat
- 10 lying on the ground back extensions
- 100 seconds of plank
