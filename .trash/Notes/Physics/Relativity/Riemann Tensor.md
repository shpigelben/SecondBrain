$$ {R^{\rho}}_{\sigma \mu \nu}  = 
\partial_{\mu} {\Gamma^{\rho}}_{\sigma \nu} - \partial_{\nu} {\Gamma^{\rho}}_{\sigma \mu} 
+{\Gamma^{\rho}}_{\mu \lambda}{\Gamma^{\lambda}}_{\sigma \nu}-{\Gamma^{\rho}}_{\nu\lambda}{\Gamma^{\lambda}}_{\sigma \mu}$$
The Riemann tensor does not vanish in [[Local Inertial Frame]], even though its locally flat. The [[Christoffel Symbols]] might vanish locally at a point $P$, but their derivative wont.
$$ {\Gamma^{\rho}}_{\mu \lambda}(P)=0 \quad \nRightarrow \quad \partial_{\mu}{\Gamma^{\rho}}_{\mu \lambda}(P)=0$$
* Curvature has dimensionality $\displaystyle\left[\frac{1}{L^{2}}\right]$ where L #why is the curvature scale
* Ricci tensor might vanish but this not necessarily implies that space is flat. It does not contain all the information the Riemann tensor does
* The Riemann tensor has $\displaystyle \frac{n^{2}(n^{2}-1)}{12}$ independent components for $N$ dimensions. (Riemann tensor for 4 dimensional space has 20 independent components)
