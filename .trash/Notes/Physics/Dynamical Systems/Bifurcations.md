#dynamical-systems 

Suppose that in addition to the dynamical variables, the [[Dynamical System#Evolution of a Dynamical System|dynamical rule]] also depends on another __control parameter__ $\lambda$. This control parameter is neither dependent nor independent dynamical variable. It is a __static__ property of the system that is changed externally and might consequently affect the behavior of the system
$$ \frac{d\mathbf{x}}{dt}=\mathbf{f}(\mathbf{x}; \lambda) $$
Our analysis of dynamical systems relies heavily on the linearization of the system around the [[Critical Points & Stability|critical points]] and so we are interested in how the stability of these points respond to a change in a certain control parameter
