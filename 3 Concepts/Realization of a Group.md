When realizing a group  $\big( \{ \tau_i \},*\big)$ we treat the elements $\tau_i$ as transformations over a space. The set of elements form a manifold, each point on the manifold is a transformation, related to a group element, that acts on certain other space.

When group elements are _realized_ to be linear transformations over a certain space we can then [[Groups | represent]] them as matrices, since matrices describe linear transformations.

$$ \tau \quad\stackrel{\small(i)}{\longmapsto}\quad U(\tau) \quad\stackrel{\small(ii)}{\longmapsto}\quad U_{ij} $$

$(i)$ - is the realization of the group
$(ii)$ - is the representation of the group
