Consider the propagation of light from a point $P_{0}$ through a medium of $n(x,y,z)$. An infinite number of rays emerge from $P_{0}$, but in general, only a finite number will pass through any other point in the medium. In special cases (lenses etc.), however, a point $P_{1}$ may be found, through which an infinity of rays pass. Such a point $P_{1}$ is said to be a sharp image of $P_{0}$.

An ideal optical instrument will sharply image every point $P_{0}$ of an object (object space) to points $P_{1}$ which constitute an image space.

Corresponding points in the two spaces are said to be conjugate.
