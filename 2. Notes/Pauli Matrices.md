The Pauli matrices are given by

$$\boxed{
\begin{align*} \\ \quad
\sigma_{x} = \begin{pmatrix}0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix} \quad \quad \sigma_{y}=\begin{pmatrix}0 & -i \\ i & 0\end{pmatrix} \quad \quad \sigma_{z}=\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1\end{pmatrix}
\quad \\\
\end{align*}}$$

Together with the identity $I$ they form a basis that spans all $2\times 2$ matrices.

#### Properties of Pauli Matrices

- Hermitian : $\large\sigma_{i}={\sigma_{i}}^{\small\dagger}$
- Unitary : $\large{\sigma_{i}^{\small-1}}={\sigma_{i}}^{\small\dagger}$
- From the two above it directly follows that $\large(\sigma_j)^2=I$
- $\det{(\sigma_{j})}=-1$
- $tr(\sigma_{j})=0$
- $\sigma_{i}\sigma_{j}=\epsilon_{ijk}\sigma_{k}$
- $[\sigma_{j},\sigma_{k}]=2i\epsilon_{jkl}\sigma_{l}$
