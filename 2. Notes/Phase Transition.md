# Necessity of Interactions
Most of the times, we treat the [[Analytic Function|non-analyticity]] of the interacting hamiltonian of the system by making what is called m [[Mean Field Approximation]] which treats the interacting part of the hamiltonian as an effective external potential. Doing so allows one to approximate the particles as independent under that effective potential and consequently build a partition function of single particle states a much easier thing to do and solve analytically.
