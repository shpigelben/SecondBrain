The energy of a photon should equal the energy gap of the levels it couples
$$h\nu=\frac{hc}{\lambda}=hc\bar{\nu} = E_{2}-E_{1}$$
 - $\nu$ - frequency
 - $\lambda$ - wavelength
 - $\bar{\nu}$ - wavenumber

![[../4 Misc/Attachments/Pasted image 20220331180018.png|500]]

If an incident photon of wavelength $\lambda$ can excite an electron from $E_{1}$ to $E_{2}$ we can say that
$$\frac{1}{\lambda}=\bar{\nu}_{2}-\bar{\nu}_{1}$$
Where $\nu_{1}$ and $\nu_{2}$ are the wavenumbers associated with $E_{1}$ and $E_{2}$ respectively
