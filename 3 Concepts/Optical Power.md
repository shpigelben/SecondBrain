Optical power is a property of a surface separating two media with different refractive indices.

The power of a round surface between two media $n_{1}$ and $n_{2}$ respectively, with a radius of curvature $R$ is given by the following.
$$
P\equiv \frac{{n_{2}-n_{1}}}{R} = n_{2}\left( \frac{n_{2}-n_{1}}{n_{2}R} \right) = \frac{n_{2}}{f}
$$
The intersection of the interface with the optical axis is called a vertex. The radius of curvature is the distance from the center of the lens to the vertex (it is positive if the center is to the right of the vertex and vice versa).
