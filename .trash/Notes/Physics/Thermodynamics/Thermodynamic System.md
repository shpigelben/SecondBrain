#TD1 #thermodynamics #equilibrium 

Thermodynamic systems are [[many body]] systems that display macroscopic behaviors that originate in microscopic phenomena. A thermodynamics system is a [[Dynamical System]] confined in space by boundaries with defined permeabilities that separate it from its surroundings.

A thermodynamic system has a set of [[State Variables]] that reflect its nature. These are tied by a [[State Function]] that describe the system in a thermodynamic equilibrium
