![InktoberTree|hw||wrap-right|gap-24|399](InktoberTree.png)
A tree drawing exercise - using contours to suggest at the 3- dimensional shape of the tree. Also using course shadow beneath the foliage to give it more volume and hint at illumination from above. 

Looking at it in hindsight - The shading disagrees and could be improved; the foliage is illuminated from above, while the trunk and branches are illuminated from the side.

Nevertheless it is a good place to start. But persistence is key for improvement.

A special resonance chamber in their heads allow for the amplification needed during reading. A near idea amongst many others in this short story. Looking forward to reading the rest.
