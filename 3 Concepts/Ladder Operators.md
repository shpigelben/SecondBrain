$$ \hat{D}\hat{X}\ket{x} = x\hat{D}\ket{x}=x\ket{x+a} $$
$$\hat{X}\hat{D}\ket{x}=\hat{X}\ket{x+a}=(x+a)\ket{x+a}$$
$$ \big(XD-DX\big)\ket{x} = \Big((x+a)-x\Big)\ket{x+a} = a\ket{x+a} = aD\ket{x} $$
$$\large\boxed{\begin{align*} \\ \quad
\big[\hat{X},\hat{D}\big] = a\hat{D} \quad \\\
\end{align*}}$$

## Normalization
$$ ||\psi || = \sqrt{\braket{\psi|\psi}} = \sqrt{\braket{x|D^{\small\dagger}D|x}} $$
