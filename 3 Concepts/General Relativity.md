___
1. [[Special Relativity]] - the speed of light is the same in all reference frames, consequently time is not universal.
2. [[ Mass | Gravitational & Inertial]] mass are equivalent - all objects (in vacuum in a certain gravitational potential) free-fall with the same acceleration, that means that the acceleration is not a property of the objects, but a geometric property of the space through which they fall.

Both of these ideas led [[Einstein]] to postulate the [[Equivalence Principle]] that states that there exists no experiment to distinguish acceleration from gravity.

>  $$\textbf{Gravity} \quad \mathbf{\equiv} \quad \textbf{Geometry}$$
