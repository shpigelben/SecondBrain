While we hear that AI is coming for our jobs and are frightened by apocalyptic prophecies, I'd like to really consider the phenomenon of AI from a bird's eye view.

Humanity has accumulated knowledge and understanding throughout the years and our methods of transcribing and preserving those insights have also improved throughout the years from the development of spoken language to the creation of the written word through the invention of the print and books and up to the digitization of knowledge and its storage in bits on a computer's memory.

Each storage method had its unique ways with which individuals communicated, learned and ultimately deepened our understanding a little bit more. Be it Einstein who learned from books or Socrates who learned and taught by verbally communicating in circles of philosophers.

I imagine the accumulated human knowledge as an artifact, greater than ourselves. A product of our societies and collective endeavors. And I imagine individual and groups interacting with this arcane element, through interfaces or access point such as libraries of the internet.

When I come at it from this perspective immediately I see in LLMs the next evolution in those access points into the collective endeavor of the human species. Trained on greater and greater chunks of what we've learned and created, these models allow us to better more naturally interact with knowledge. LLMs in essence bring us nearer the human achievement, and if used correctly, allow us to nudge humanity yet further.

The risks are :
1. letting LLM outputs pollute what we consider pure human product.
2. giving them further agentic access to the physical we inhabit. For it they are truly of agentic capacity and possess desires and goals. More likely than not our aims are at odds at which point conflict and chaos will arise.

Another risk is using that gate-way, that access point into human knowledge merely as a tool for getting information. If we outsource our understanding and deep processing of this information to LLMs not only are we allowing them to pollute our pool of knowledge with artificial creation over which we have no rule, but we are also limiting our capacity to create and innovate, while at them same time giving more capabilities and agency to these alien artifacts we call AIs.

Human Interaction with the Physical World → Mental Models → Platonic Insights → LLMs
