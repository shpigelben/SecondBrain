I have a presentation to give at work today. As is usual with me, I’ve began stressing about it from yesterday. Every time I think about it my heart rate climbs up and I can’t focus and think clearly. I know this fear is irrational, I know it and I’ve given more than several talks before and always I seem to spiral into this thought loop of  which I can’t seem to get over.

I recall reading in the [[Will of the Few]] about Vis’ father telling him when he gets anxious before speaking in front of people how this unpleasant feeling is something to embrace rather than repel, because it shows there’s room for growth. I like that Idea, but it alone won’t solve this issue.

Taking a few deep breaths and telling myself that there is nothing to fear really and that afterwards everything will go back to normal
