> [!NOTE] Title
Hydrostatics is the absence of tangential forces (to any surface they act upon)


$$\begin{align*}
\mathbf{F}&= \int\limits_{V_{0}}\mathbf{f} \ dV= -\oint\limits_{\partial V_{0}} P d\mathbf{A} = -\left[\oint\limits_{\partial V_{0}} (P \mathbf{n}) \cdot d\mathbf{A}\right]\mathbf{n}\\
& =-\left[\int\limits_{V_{0}}\nabla\cdot(P\mathbf{n})dV\right]\mathbf{n} =
 \int\limits_{V_{0}} (-\boldsymbol{\nabla P})dV
\end{align*} \tag{3}$$
$$\mathbf{f} = -\boldsymbol{\nabla P} \tag{4}$$
The differential force in a volume element $dV$ due to the the pressure inside that volume is given by equation $(4)$ while for a total volume.
