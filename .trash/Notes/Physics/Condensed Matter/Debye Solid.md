- An improvement upon the [[Einstein Solid]] model, Debye also treated the atoms in a solid as [[Quantum Harmonic Oscillator| |quantum harmonic oscilators]], but not as independent ones.
<br>
- The interaction of the oscillators allow for traveling sound waves in the solid.
<br>
- Where Einstein's model assumes constant and equal frequency for all atoms, Debye's treatment integrates over all possible modes in a [[Brillouin Zone]]. 
<br>
- Debye's treatment of the modes in the solid is equivalent to Planks [[Black Body Radiation | black body radiation]], where instead of photons of the EM field there are [[Phonons]] which are the vibrational modes of the ions in the solid.
