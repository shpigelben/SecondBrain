# Hubble

The expansion of the universe was first concluded from the [[FRW Metric]] solution to the [[Einstein Field Equation|Einstein equation]], and was later experimentally confirmed by Hubble. Hubble's law is born out of the observation that __the receding speed__ of galaxies away from each other is proportional to their distance from one another

$$
\dot{r} = Hr
$$

Where the proportionality constant is known as __Hubble constant__. 

___

If $H$ is constant in time then $\dot{r}=Hr\Rightarrow r=Ce^{Ht}$

if $H\propto \frac{2}{3t}$ then $\frac{dr}{dt}=\frac{2}{3} \frac{dt}{t}\Rightarrow r(t)=Ct^{2/3}$ 

___

Can things travels faster than light ? According to Hubble - Yes. Since receding speed is proportional to velocity, assuming the universe is large enough, there is a distance from our point of view $r_{\tiny H}$ such that

$$
c = Hr_{\tiny H} \Rightarrow r_{\tiny H} = \frac{c}{H_{0}} \approx \frac{3\times 10^{10}}{2\times 10^{-18}}\approx 10^{28} \text{cm}\approx 3\text{Gpc}
$$

This is the size of the observable universe. Further points travel faster than light relative to the observer, and therefore light from them cannot reach it.
