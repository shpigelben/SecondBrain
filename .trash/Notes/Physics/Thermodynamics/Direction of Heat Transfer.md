#TD1 #thermodynamics 

Consider a system composed of two subsystems that are not in mutual [[Thermodynamic Equilibrium]], it directly implies that the composite system is not in internal TDE.

Assuming the systems are in thermal contact, there will be a [[Thermodynamic Processes|spontaneous process]] during the systems will exchange heat, without loss of generality we assume heat flows from subsystems $2\to1$

$$dU_{1}=+\delta Q \  \ \leadsto \ \  dU_{2}=-\delta Q$$

The total change in [[Entropy]] (due to additivity) is the sum of the changes in entropy of the subsystems, and taking the [[Second Law of Thermodynamics]] (for spontaneous irreversible processes) into account, we have

$$0<dS = dS_{1} + dS_{2} = \frac{\delta Q}{T_{1}} - \frac{\delta Q}{T_{2}}$$

The above leads directly to the fact that $\large T_{2}\gt T_{1}$. We thus conclude that heat flows from systems with higher temperature to systems with lower temperature.
